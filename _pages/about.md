I am Jaeho Cho, a researcher at the Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST).
My research focuses on **multimodal industrial anomaly detection** and **self-supervised representation learning**.
In particular, I study how embedding-predictive (JEPA-style) objectives can adapt and refine RGB and 3D point-cloud features
for detecting and localizing defects in industrial inspection.
